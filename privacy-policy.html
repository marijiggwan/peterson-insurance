Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Peterson Insurance Group respects the privacy of every individual who uses this website and our services.

Website Privacy

Your e-mail address is obtained for internal purposes only (as it relates to insurance policies) and will not be sold or shared with any unaffiliated third party other than as set forth in this Privacy Policy.

Peterson Insurance Group collects personal information from you (such as your name, e-mail address, and other data) when you provide personal data through forms on this website. If you send us e-mails, we may from time to time use information about your preferences and interests to send updates to you about Peterson Insurance Group services.

Our website includes links to other websites. Those sites may have different privacy policies. Peterson Insurance Group is not responsible for the privacy policies of other sites.

This website is directed to adults and is not directed to children under the age of 18. Peterson Insurance Group complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act and does not permit registration by, and will not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from, anyone under 13.

SMS Communications

By opting in to receive SMS communications from Peterson Insurance Group Inc, you consent to the practices described here. We may collect your mobile phone number, your name, and any other information you provide during the opt-in process, along with basic usage data about how you interact with our messages.

We don't share your information with any third party for any reason at any time. No mobile information will be shared with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.

We use this information to send the SMS messages you've opted in to receive, to improve our communications, and to comply with applicable laws and industry standards. Message and data rates may apply, and message frequency may vary.

Opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message — you'll receive a confirmation and be removed from our SMS list. Reply HELP for assistance, or contact us directly at (828) 645-8777. Texting HELP will not affect your subscription status.

Data Security & Disclosure

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, alteration, and disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to outside parties without your consent, except as required by law, in response to valid legal requests, or as necessary to provide our services (including service providers who assist us in operating this website).

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes are effective immediately upon posting the revised policy. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
