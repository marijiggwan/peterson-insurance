Our Weaverville
Community

Our Weaverville community is brimming with stunning beauty, exciting events, and talented artists and musicians. At Peterson Insurance Group, we prioritize supporting our local businesses, community gatherings, and dedicated nonprofits addressing the needs of those less fortunate.

Weaverville 2026 Events

Art in Autumn — September 19, 2026

Saturday, September 19, 2026 · 10am – 6pm · Main Street, Downtown Weaverville

2026 is the 19th annual Art in Autumn Outdoor Arts and Crafts Festival. This much-loved event is held on Main Street in downtown Weaverville, North Carolina — in Buncombe County, just 7 miles from downtown Asheville.

The event features over 100 juried artists and craftspeople, plus live music performed by beloved local artists. Each year, a guest judge from the local art scene awards $1,000 for Best of Show, $500 for second place, $250 for third, and four $50 honorable mentions.
